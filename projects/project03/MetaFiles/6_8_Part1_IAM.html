Notes for DP-7 review
• Role lab-project-authenticate-role matches the contract glob lab-project-*; created WITH permissions_boundary = arn:aws:iam::772360735396:policy/LabProjectPermissionsBoundary.
• Trust = lambda.amazonaws.com; managed policy = AWSLambdaBasicExecutionRole (CloudWatch logs only). No VPC policy (public RDS).
• Lambda → RDS uses NO IAM — MySQL username/password (authsvc-read-write) carried in the packaged authsvc-config.ini.
• Claude-Conjurer (PowerUserAccess + ClaudeConjurerPlane2IAMDelegation) is permitted to create lab-project-* roles + PassRole to lambda.amazonaws.com per the IAM contract.
